\caption{The two disclosure settings of HE-OFT, sharing the encrypted aggregate $\mathsf{Enc}(\theta^\star)$
produced in \cref{fig:protocol}. \emph{Release} threshold-decrypts $\theta^\star$ once and hands every client
the plaintext model. \emph{Serve} never decrypts it: an untrusted serving party --- holding only the public
and evaluation keys --- answers a query $x$ by running the frozen backbone in plaintext, mapping the features
through the encrypted head at multiplicative depth one, and taking the $\arg\max$ under encryption; a threshold
of clients then decrypts only the predicted label. The model never appears in the clear and a querier learns
only labels. The encrypted $\arg\max$ is the one operation above depth one; a log-depth SIMD tournament
(QuickMax~\cite{zhang2025nexus}, over homomorphic comparison~\cite{cheon2020comparison}) evaluates it in
$\lceil\log_2\Cc\rceil$ rounds, and because homomorphic evaluation is identical under a collectively generated
key, these single-key costs transfer directly.}
