\caption{Step-by-step view of HE-OFT. There is no alignment phase: the backbone and the initializer
$\theta_0$ are public and carry no data (step~1). Each client fine-tunes in plaintext and encrypts only its
displacement (steps~2--3); the server's sole operation is the depth-one candidate aggregation (step~4),
performed without the ability to decrypt; a quorum of clients decrypts and votes (step~5). The selected model
$\theta^\star$ is then either \emph{released} (decrypted and distributed) or \emph{served} (kept encrypted and
queried under encryption) --- the two disclosure settings of \cref{sec:serve} (step~6).}
