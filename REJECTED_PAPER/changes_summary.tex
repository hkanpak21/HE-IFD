{\large\bfseries Summary of Revisions: FedDil $\rightarrow$ HE-IFD}\\[1pt]
{\footnotesize Manuscript TDSCSI-2026-04-1278 (rejected) $\rightarrow$ resubmission. What changed, and how each
reviewer concern is addressed (R$i$-W/Q $=$ Reviewer~$i$ weakness/question).}

\textbf{Principal change --- a method redesign, not a patch.} The submitted version trained an
encryption-friendly model \emph{under} homomorphic encryption, replacing activations with polynomial
approximations and transmitting large encrypted objects over many internal steps; this was the source of the
accuracy loss, the $\sim$460\,GB per-client upload, and the degradation at higher client counts. The
resubmission performs \emph{no} training under encryption: each client fine-tunes a small low-rank adapter on a
frozen, public, pretrained backbone in plaintext and uploads only the encrypted parameter displacement, and the
server's sole cryptographic operation is one sample-weighted linear sum under multiparty CKKS at
\emph{multiplicative depth one}, decrypted by a threshold of clients. Freezing the adapter's down-projection
makes this aggregate \emph{exact} task arithmetic, so the encrypted object is $19$ ciphertexts ($9.5$\,MiB),
sent once. This resolves the reviewers' concerns at their root rather than mitigating them.

\textbf{What changed.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \rc{No encrypted training:} the only encrypted operation is a depth-one linear combine (PT$\times$CT,
CT$+$CT); it reproduces the plaintext aggregate to relative $\ell_2\!\approx\!10^{-9}$ (zero accuracy cost),
with no polynomial activations and no bootstrapping.
\item \rc{One-shot, fine-tuning-based:} a single upload and download; learning is local and in plaintext on a
frozen pretrained backbone, the setting the encryption constraint admits.
\item \rc{Cryptographic, not differentially private:} contributions are protected without perturbation; only
the final shared model is revealed, and its residual leakage is measured.
\item \rc{New contribution:} encrypted multi-candidate release with post-decryption client-side selection,
which also yields a measured defense against a poisoning client at no homomorphic cost.
\item \rc{Evaluation and exposition:} language (RoBERTa), vision (ViT-B/16), and a $0.5$B language model, with
five ablation axes and matched comparators; end-to-end multiparty CKKS measured in Lattigo; prose rewritten
plainly, new figures, and the pseudocode replaced by formal equations.
\end{itemize}
